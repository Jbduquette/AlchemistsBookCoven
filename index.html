Your host

Hosted by Lili Duquette

Reader of everything. Rewatcher of nothing.

Every month, Lili picks a book. Sometimes it's a classic, sometimes something they found face-down in a used bookstore. Listeners read along, and the episode is one honest conversation about whether the book actually worked.

Before this podcast, Lili was the friend who texted "chapter 14, we need to talk" at 1am. Now that energy has a microphone.

No syllabus, no homework, no gatekeeping.

Your host

Hosted by Lili Duquette

Reader of everything · Rewatcher of nothing

Every month, Lili picks a book — sometimes a classic, sometimes something they found face-down in a used bookstore — and invites listeners to read along. No syllabus, no homework, just one honest conversation about whether the book actually worked.

Before this podcast, Lili was the friend who texted "chapter 14, we need to talk" at 1am. Now that energy has a microphone.
